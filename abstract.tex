\noindent This work presents the current efforts to constrain nuclear physics parameters given the precision of up to date telescopes and gravitational wave deterctors. We focus on the equation of state of dense matter and has implications for the waveforms and electromagnetic signals from neutron star mergers. we aim to provide tighter constraints on the symmetry energy and its density dependence. Our analysis includes a comprehensive review of recent observational results, theoretical models, and statistical methods used to extract information about nuclear matter properties from astrophysical data.

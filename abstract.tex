\noindent Binary neutron stars mergers are interesting astrophysical events where different physical phenomena at different scales meets. 
General relativity with equation of state of nuclear matter dictate the hydrodynamic evolution while the neutrino transport and the nuclear statistical equilibrium stablish the composition. 
Once the temeprature has drop the abundance and thermodynamic evolution of the ejected material will be given by the reaction network predicting the r-process nucleo synthesis and its consequence nuclear heating.
